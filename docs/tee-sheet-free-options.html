Free places, quieter

All three use the lighter navies you picked. Each shows a completely free four-ball and times with one, two or three spaces left.

A · One line for a free four-ball

A free four-ball has no boxes, just “All 4 free · tap to book” across the row. Times with someone in them keep “+ Space” in each gap.

B · Just a gold +

Every free place shows a single gold “+”. No words, so a free four-ball reads as four quiet plus signs. The row colour already says it’s free.

C · Count the spaces once

The first gap says how many places are left (“+ 2 spaces”) and the other gaps stay empty. A free four-ball says “All 4 free” on one line.
