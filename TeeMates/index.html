TeeMates

A clickable mockup. Course images come from the Ailsa yardage guide; everything else is sample data.

  1. Scores: pick a game from the drop-down, choose pairs for better-ball, then score hole by hole.
  2. Leaderboard: today's field by gross, net, Stableford or birdies, updating as you score.
  3. Course: the yardage book for every hole, with today's pins.
  4. Admin: tee times, bookings, buddies, pins, games, guest points and team events.
