Count this round?

When a scorecard opens and someone on it is in the Winter League, TeeMate asks straight away whether today's Stableford counts for their team.

One round a week counts. Players who've already counted a round this week are shown but can't be ticked. The answer can be changed until hole 1 is saved.

Try the Yes / No buttons and “Done” on the phone. “Ask me later” closes it; the gold line on the card reopens it.
