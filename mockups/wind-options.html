Wind on the map

Three ideas, drawn on a stand-in map, using tonight's wind: 17 mph from the west.

A · Lots of small arrows

A light grid of arrows across the whole map, all pointing the way the wind blows. Calm, easy to read, never covers the course.

B · Moving wind streaks

Faint lines drift across the map with the wind, faster when it's stronger. The most alive, but busier.

C · Compass in the corner

A clean map with a small badge showing speed and direction. Tidiest, but the wind isn't drawn on the course.

A + C · Arrows with the badge

The small arrows across the map, plus the speed and direction in the corner.
