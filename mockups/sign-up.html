Simpler sign-up

The first screen shows two clear buttons: I'm new or I've got an account.

Signing up is one page: name, email and password. That sends the request to the admin and keeps you signed in.

While you wait, the app checks for approval every few seconds. The moment the admin approves, it goes straight into TeeMate without asking you to sign in again. If you close it and come back later, it opens straight in too.

If your email is already on the members list, you skip the waiting step entirely.

Tap through on the phone too. On the waiting screen, “Simulate admin approves” stands in for the admin tapping Approve.
